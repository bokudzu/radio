    About the project

    Kyiv Contemporary Music Days (KCMD) and Radio Tallinn 1965 launch a joint project: a KCMD channel on Väin, the new media platform, launched at the Huddersfield Contemporary Music Festival in the UK, 20–29 November 2026.

    The channel is a place where Ukrainian musicians and their friends, scattered across Ukraine and abroad, can always hear the voices of their own and join in. It brings together broadcasts curated by artists, recordings of KCMD concerts over the years, improvised sets, generative streams and conversations, and stays open to new formats as it evolves over time.

    KCMD also brings TACET to the whole platform: whenever there is an air raid alert in Kyiv, all channels on Väin fall silent, and when it ends, a 15-second signal by a Ukrainian artist breaks the silence.
