    About the project

    Kyiv Contemporary Music Days (KCMD), a platform for new music from Kyiv, and Radio Tallinn 1965, with technical support from POSITRON.STUDIO, have joined forces on a new project: a KCMD channel on V2in, the new radio platform presented at the Huddersfield Contemporary Music Festival (hcmf//) in the UK, 20–29 November 2026.

    The channel is a place where Ukrainian musicians and their friends, scattered across Ukraine and abroad, can always hear each other’s voices and join in themselves. It brings together broadcasts curated by artists, recordings of KCMD concerts over the years, improvised sets, generative streams and conversations, and will remain open to new formats as it evolves over time.

    KCMD also brings TACET to the entire V2in platform: TACET means that whenever there is an air raid alert in Kyiv, all channels on V2in fall silent, and when the air raid ends, a 30-second signal by a Ukrainian artist breaks the silence, and then each channel returns to its programme.
